# Trademark policy

The code in this repository is licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0. The name
**Permaura™** and the Permaura logo are not. They are trademarks of Pixelite SL, and section 6 of
the Apache License explicitly withholds any right to use them.

That split is deliberate and it is the ordinary arrangement for this kind of project. Mozilla
licenses Firefox's source freely but holds the name, which is why Debian shipped the same browser
for years as Iceweasel. Rust and Kubernetes both run versions of the same policy. We are not doing
anything unusual to you here.

The reason is narrow. The code is yours to build on, change and ship. The name is how somebody
knows who is answering when the thing holding their production credentials does something
unexpected. Keeping those two separate is what makes the first one safe to give away.

## You do not need to ask us

- Say that your product, plugin, connector, article or talk **works with**, is **built on**, or is
  **compatible with** Permaura. Describing a true relationship is always fine, and you never need
  our permission to do it.
- Use the name to refer to this project: documentation, blog posts, conference talks, comparison
  tables, benchmarks and reviews, including unflattering ones.
- Redistribute unmodified official builds under the Permaura name, and package them for a
  distribution or a package manager.
- Use the logo to link to us or to illustrate a piece about us, without stretching, recoloring or
  redrawing it.

## Please pick your own name

- **A fork, or any build carrying your changes**, shipped to other people. Give it a different
  name. You are welcome, and encouraged, to say it is "a fork of Permaura" or "based on Permaura"
  underneath your own.
- **A hosted or commercial service** built on this code. The service needs its own name, even
  though the engine inside it is ours.
- **Anything that reads as coming from us**: a product whose name starts with "Permaura", a domain
  containing it, a social account, an app-store listing, or a logo derived from ours.

"Compatible with Permaura" is fine. "Permaura" as the name of your product is not. That is
essentially the whole policy.

## The test

Would a reasonable person think we made it, shipped it, or vouched for it? If they would, and we
did not, that is what this document exists to prevent. Everything else is fine.

## Notes

We use ™ rather than ®. The mark is in use and unregistered, and ™ signals a claim without
requiring a registration. Do not write Permaura®: claiming a registration that does not exist is
an offence in a number of countries, and it would be our problem as much as yours.

If you want to do something this policy does not cover, ask at `hello@permaura.com` rather than
guessing. We would much rather say yes to a specific request.
