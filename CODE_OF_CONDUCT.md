# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the person you are replying to is acting in good faith and knows something you do
not. Disagree about the work rather than about each other.

## What is expected

Permaura is a security project, which means a lot of its conversations are people saying "this is
wrong" to each other. That is the job, and it is welcome. What makes it work is keeping the criticism
pointed at the code, the design or the claim, and not at the person who wrote it.

Concretely:

- Say what is wrong and why. "This leaks the token on the redirect path" is useful. "This is amateur
  hour" is not, and it is also less specific.
- Take a correction the way you would want yours taken. Being wrong in public is the ordinary cost of
  working in public.
- Respect a maintainer's no. Not every good idea belongs in this project, and a closed issue is not an
  insult.
- English is not everybody's first language. Read past the phrasing.

## What is not

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sexualized content, deliberate intimidation,
publishing someone's private information, and sustained disruption of a discussion. None of it is
tolerated, whatever the technical merit of the person doing it.

## Security reports are different

Do not open a public issue for a vulnerability. [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) has the disclosure
process, and following it is part of behaving well here: a public report on a live flaw puts users at
risk while everybody argues about severity.

## Enforcement

Report anything that needs attention to **hello@permaura.com**. Reports go to the maintainers and are
handled privately. You will get an acknowledgment, and you will not be asked to justify why the
behavior bothered you.

Responses range from a private word to a permanent ban, depending on severity and on whether it has
happened before. Maintainers who do not hold themselves to this face more consequence than
contributors, not less.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, in issues and pull requests, and anywhere someone is representing the
project in public.
